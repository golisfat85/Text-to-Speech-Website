How voice packs work

Three steps. About a minute.

01

Buy and download

You'll get a download link straight away: a folder of recordings for each voice in the pack.

02

Add voice

In Cadence, open the voice menu, choose Add voice, name it, and select that voice's recordings.

03

Start speaking

The voice is ready immediately, alongside your others. Use it as much as you like.

Do I need Cadence to use a voice pack?

Yes. Voice packs add voices to Cadence. Get Cadence first if you haven't already.

Can I use pack voices commercially?

Yes. Audio you make with a voice pack can be used in your own projects, including commercial ones. Each pack includes its licence; please don't use a voice to impersonate the person behind it.

Where do the voices come from?

Every pack voice is recorded by a real person who has agreed to have their voice used this way.
