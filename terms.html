What you make

Audio you create with Cadence is yours to use, including commercially, provided you have the right to use the text and the voice.

Voices and consent

Only add voices of people who have given you permission. Don't use Cadence to impersonate anyone, to deceive or defraud, or to make content that is unlawful, harassing or harmful. We may refuse service to anyone who misuses it.
